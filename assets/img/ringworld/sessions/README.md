Photos from ringworld sessions.

Drop a photo in here and point a session at it with `photo:` in
`_data/ringworld.yml` — that turns the session's card into a flip card, with the
character on the front and the photo on the back, revealed on hover. Add
`photo_alt` to describe it.

Filenames must not start with `_`; Jekyll skips those, and the card flips to a
broken image.
